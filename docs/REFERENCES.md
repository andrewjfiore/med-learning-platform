## Verification Notes

### DCML Pathway
- **Decussation**: Internal arcuate fibers at caudal medulla ✅ (StatPearls, Kenhub, Physiopedia, Wikipedia all agree)
- **1st order**: DRG → nucleus gracilis/cuneatus ✅
- **2nd order**: Medulla nuclei → VPL thalamus ✅
- **3rd order**: VPL → somatosensory cortex ✅
- **Gracilis = legs (medial), Cuneatus = arms (lateral)** ✅

### Corticospinal Tract
- **Decussation**: Pyramidal decussation at caudal medulla ✅
- **Percentage**: Sources vary 75-90% (StatPearls says "75-90%"), commonly cited as ~85-90%. First Aid says "85%". App says "85-90%" ✅ acceptable range
- **Lateral CST = crossed (major), Anterior CST = uncrossed (minor)** ✅

### Spinothalamic Tract
- **Decussation**: Anterior white commissure, 1-2 levels above entry ✅ (standard teaching per First Aid + multiple sources)

### CST Percentage Discrepancy
- Kenhub says 80%, StatPearls says 75-90%, IMAIOS says ~90%, First Aid says 85%
- **Resolution**: Using "85-90%" as primary (First Aid + StatPearls consensus), noting range is 75-90%
